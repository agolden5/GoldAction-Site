What we do

Golden Intelligence LLC provides an automated missed-call text-back SMS service for small businesses. When a customer calls one of our client businesses and the call goes unanswered or reaches voicemail, our system automatically sends that caller a text message on the business's behalf — acknowledging the missed call and inviting them to reply by text. This gives the business a chance to respond in the moment, so the caller stays engaged instead of hanging up and calling a competitor. Messages are sent only in response to a call the customer placed to the business, and every message identifies the business it is sent for and includes instructions to opt out.

How it works

Three steps, with no app for your customer to install and nothing for your staff to remember.

1

A call goes unanswered

A customer calls your business and nobody is able to pick up, or the call reaches voicemail.

2

We text them back

Within seconds the caller receives a text from your business number, letting them know you saw the call and can help by text.

3

The conversation continues

They reply, you respond, and the customer stays with you instead of moving on to the next business on their list.

Messaging policy

Our messages are replies, not campaigns. We do not send marketing or promotional blasts, and we never message a number that has not called one of our client businesses.

Why you received a message

You called a business that uses our service and the call was not answered. The text is a direct reply to that call.

Stopping messages

Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe, or HELP for support information.

Message frequency

Varies, and depends on how often you call the business. Message and data rates may apply.

Your data

We do not sell or rent phone numbers or message content. See our Privacy Policy for full detail.
