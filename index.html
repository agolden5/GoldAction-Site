What we do

Golden Intelligence LLC provides an automated missed-call text-back SMS service for small businesses. When a customer calls one of our client businesses and the call goes unanswered or reaches voicemail, our system automatically sends that caller a text message on the business's behalf — acknowledging the missed call and including a link to book, order, or contact the business directly. This gives the caller an immediate way to act, so they stay engaged instead of hanging up and calling a competitor. Messages are sent only in response to a call the customer placed to the business, and every message identifies the business it is sent for and includes instructions to opt out.

How it works

Three steps, with no app for your customer to install and nothing for your staff to remember.

1

A call goes unanswered

A customer calls your business and nobody is able to pick up, or the call reaches voicemail.

2

3

They book or get in touch

The link takes them straight to your booking, ordering, or contact page — so the lead stays with you instead of moving on to the next business on their list.

Messaging policy

Our messages are replies, not campaigns. We do not send marketing or promotional blasts, and we never message a number that has not called one of our client businesses. A message may include a link to a business-specific action page so you can book, order, or get in touch without waiting on hold.

Why you received a message

You called a business that uses our service and the call was not answered. The text is a direct reply to that call, and may include a link to a business-specific action page — for example booking, ordering, or contact — alongside the acknowledgement of your missed call.

Stopping messages

Reply STOP, STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, or QUIT at any time to unsubscribe, or HELP for information about this service.

Message frequency

One message per missed call. We do not send recurring or scheduled messages. Message and data rates may apply.

Your data
