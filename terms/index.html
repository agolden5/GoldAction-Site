1. The Service

The Service connects to a subscribing business's telephone line and monitors for inbound calls that go unanswered or reach voicemail. When such a call is detected, the Service automatically sends a text message from the business's messaging number to the number that placed the call. The message acknowledges the missed call and invites the caller to reply by text. Replies are routed to the business, and the business can respond to the caller through the Service.

The Service is a communications tool. It does not answer calls, it does not speak with callers, and it does not make representations to callers on the business's behalf beyond the message template the business has configured and approved.
